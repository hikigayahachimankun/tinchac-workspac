# AI use and responsibility

This is a solo student project with AI-assisted engineering. The original four-role plan names responsibilities, not four recruited people. Recovery maintainer automated code review, tests, integration, draft documentation, public-input worksheets and OCR rendering. These are technical self-checks, not independent human review.

Application live inference is designed for Gemini extraction/verification through a budgeted adapter. Recovery made **0 paid Gemini calls**; account preflight and model quality remain unverified. Tesseract performs local OCR; retrieval is local FTS5/character TF-IDF. Fixture outputs and replay are labeled explicitly.

No AI output has been presented as human gold, independent evidence scoring, survey responses, consent or real benchmark performance. Dev cases are source-derived/synthetically modified drafts. Claim scope, human annotation and evidence sufficiency require actual source review and named human accountability. No claim of measured fraud prevention or institutional endorsement is made.

Dependencies are pinned in requirements.lock.txt/frontend/package-lock.json. Record upstream licences and code/data reuse in release review; publicly readable DUT pages do not establish redistribution rights. Raw/candidate images and personal data are excluded from published artifacts. PyMuPDF redistribution terms remain a release gate; local development is not licence clearance. The project owner supplies identity/permission statements directly to the competition, never through fabricated AI declarations.
